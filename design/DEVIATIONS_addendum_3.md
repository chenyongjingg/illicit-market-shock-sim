## Why a third volume

Volumes 1 and 2 are sealed — volume 1 at `2026-11-01T06:58:35Z` (sha `353930c3…`, 148,023 B, 88
entries) and volume 2 after the corrections recorded in its own seal history (sha `bded231b…`,
8,501 B, 2 entries). Neither is edited here. Editing a sealed volume would turn the sha a reader
is holding into a label rather than a record, which is the defect volume 2 exists to avoid.

This volume covers a **separate execution window** that opened after volume 2 was sealed: three
pre-registered experimental packages, described in `runs/exp/<id>/prereg.yaml`, run under a task
assignment that forbade re-running any existing stage and forbade any change to criteria,
thresholds, scenario grid or subnet definitions. All artefacts are under `runs/exp/`.
